# KOMPOSOS-AML

Score-first anti-money-laundering detection on IBM's synthetic AML benchmark
(HI-Small: 5,078,345 transactions, 515,088 accounts, 1.23% of accounts touched
by flagged activity). Successor to KOMPOSOS-SEC, which spent a year building
detection methods before it could score any of them. This repo inverts the
order: **every idea is scored against a held-out labeled split, with controls,
on the day it is written.** A losing result is still a result — the running
log of all of them is [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md).

## Headline numbers (always quoted as a pair)

- **0.8991 AUROC / 87.4% precision@100** on HI-Small (5-seed means; base rate
  1.23% — that top-100 is a 71x lift).
- **0.8075 AUROC / 47.0% precision@100** with the *same recipe, frozen, zero
  re-tuning* on true LI-Small — a different generation run (62.6x lift over
  its 0.75% base rate).
- **0.8736 AUROC / 76.8% precision@1000** frozen on HI-Medium (31.9M rows,
  2.08M accounts) — the full pipeline runs on a 32 GB laptop, and the
  cross-currency triage tier is 99.6% pure there (one seed: 435/435).

The gap between those two is measured, decomposed, and mostly the label's
composition: 78% of LI-Small's positive accounts are structureless
"background" laundering that no structural method can see (HI-Small: 50%).
Per-typology skill ordering transfers exactly across datasets. Full
decomposition in FINDINGS.md's Phase 3 section.

## What the method is

Nothing exotic — that is the finding:

1. **Counting features** (degree, amounts, transaction counts, banks, cycles).
2. **Damped collaborative filtering** over shared counterparties, propagating
   train-set labels only (transductive; no test-label leakage).
3. **Hub exclusion**: the 15 giant institutional counterparties (popularity
   567–14,775 vs ≤93 for the other 515,073 — a literal empty gap in the
   distribution) are dropped from the CF matrix. This fixed a measured defect
   where hub-only accounts tied at the top of the ranking (precision@100 was
   4.25% *while precision@1000 was 38.9%*).
4. **A binary cross-currency flag** (does the account do FX at all).
5. Logistic regression over the above.

Elaborate alternatives were scored and lost, repeatedly: PageRank, Kan
extensions, Dempster-Shafer as a ranker, amount-weighted everything (four
independent negatives), 2-hop CF, persistent-homology-adjacent coherence.
The catalog of tried/untried ideas is [MATH_IDEAS.md](MATH_IDEAS.md).

## The discipline

Every scored method gets, in the same run: a fixed held-out split (60/40
stratified, seed 0, identical account ordering across every script), a
shuffled-label control (must collapse to ~0.5 AUROC), a distinct-score count
(catches universal-tie bugs), precision at operational k (100/500/1000 — a
single aggregate metric once hid a defect that made the top of the worklist
useless), and 5-seed robustness before any number becomes a headline.
No try/except around scoring paths. No time-window features (a window tuned
until a signal appears is a threshold in disguise).

## Honest caveats

- **Synthetic data.** Generated by IBM's AMLworld simulator. Ground-truth
  typology audits (`phase3_patterns_audit.py`) show ~50% of flagged accounts
  are background noise with no structure, and two typologies (STACK,
  BIPARTITE) are account-level-invisible by construction — measured ceilings,
  documented in FINDINGS.md.
- **Transductive.** CF uses the full graph structure plus train labels.
  Verified not to be mere "shares a component with a known positive"
  (`phase2_cf_verify.py`).

## Reproducing

Data goes in `data/` (not in the repo — see NOTICE for attribution):

- HI-Small, no login needed:
  `https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/eexzzm/IBM-Transactions-for-Anti-Money-Laundering-HI-Small-Trans/parquet/default/train/0.parquet` (+ `1.parquet`)
  saved as `data/hi_small_0.parquet`, `data/hi_small_1.parquet`.
- Everything else (LI-Small, the Medium/Large variants, and the
  `*_Patterns.txt` typology ground-truth files): the Kaggle archive
  `ealtman2019/ibm-transactions-for-anti-money-laundering-aml`.

Then, in order: `phase0_inspect.py` (label sanity), `phase1_*.py` (counting
baselines), `phase2_*.py` (methods; `phase2_invariants.py` is the operational
best), `phase3_*.py` (transfer + typology ground truth), `phase4_*.py`
(blind-spot attempts, both negative, both explained), `phase5_*.py`
(HI-Medium scale rung). Python 3.10, pandas, scipy, scikit-learn.
